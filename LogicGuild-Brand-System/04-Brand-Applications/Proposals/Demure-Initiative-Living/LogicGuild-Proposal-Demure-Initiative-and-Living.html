1. Project Overview

We propose designing and developing two modern, professional, and fully responsive websites tailored specifically to Demure Initiative and Demure Living.

Our goal is to strengthen both brands' digital presence, improve customer experience, simplify online payments with Paystack, and create easy-to-manage platforms that support your long-term business growth.

3. Easy-to-Use Admin Panel (Included for Both Websites)

To make managing your websites simple and straightforward, both Demure Initiative and Demure Living will include a secure, user-friendly admin control panel so your team can easily handle day-to-day operations:

Manage Products & Prices
Easily add new products, update pictures and descriptions, adjust prices, set bundle deals, and track available stock levels.
Product Bidding Management (Demure Initiative)
Set starting prices and time limits for bidding items, see bids as they come in live, see the winning bidder when time ends, and let them pay via Paystack.
Orders & Payment Tracking
View customer orders instantly, verify payments through Paystack, and update order statuses (Processing, Shipped, Delivered).
Update Website Content Anytime
Easily update your text, banners, charity project stories, and contact info whenever you like, without needing to hire a programmer.
Customer Details & Records
Keep an organized record of your customers, their contact details, past purchases, and bidding participation in one tidy list.
Secure Staff Login
Password-protected admin accounts so only authorized team members can access your website dashboard.
